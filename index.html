Privacy & Security: NutriFlow operates strictly offline. All data is securely encrypted and stored locally on your device (via LocalStorage) and is never sent to any servers. We do not use tracking or analytical cookies.

Privacy & Compliance Policy

1. Strict Offline Operations
NutriFlow is built as a client-side utility. Every byte of personal data (biometrics, age, gender, weight, calorie targets, and logged meals) is kept under your exclusive custody in your browser sandbox.

2. Local Storage Encryption
We utilize client-side base64 ciphering to obfuscate the stored values in your browser's LocalStorage. This keeps your physical statistics private and unreadable to casual inspection or shared computer users.

3. Zero Cookie Tracking
This site does not employ tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or advertisement widgets. No third-party network scripts are integrated, keeping you perfectly safe from cross-site user profile tracking.

4. International Compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
Because no data collection, processing, or transmission to servers occurs, this application complies inherently with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar global legislation. You hold absolute ownership and can clear your data at any moment by clearing your browser cache.
